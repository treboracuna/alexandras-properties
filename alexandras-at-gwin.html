Our Story

A Legacy Sixty Years
in the Making

Alexandra's at Gwin is the first property in the Alexandra's portfolio. Sixty years of a father's dream, now open for stays, events, and celebrations.

A father dreamed of owning this land for over sixty years. His son Robert Acuña made that dream real and transformed it into Alexandra's at Gwin. Just minutes from the city, on private acreage with a pool, cabin, and pavilion. A place to unwind, reset, and return ready.
