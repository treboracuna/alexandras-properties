Ezra

What would it feel like to know?

Not hope. Not probably fine. Know. Every owner, and every operator working alone, knows the quiet weight of a client list that is technically complete and possibly wrong. What has that weight cost over the years, and who would ever say? Ezra was built inside a working business to set it down for good.

01

Records in. Answers out.

Every client, every commitment, every payment lands in one place and is held against what was actually promised. Three answers come back: what is settled, what needs a look, and what fell through. The fog over the month lifts, and what is left is a number that holds.

Nothing is trusted until it matches the record. Not the summary. Not the spreadsheet. Not the feeling that it is probably fine.

The fog lifts
02

Who earned it. Who got paid for it.

A system knows whose name is on the account. It does not know who sat at the kitchen table and earned it. When those two names differ, someone is quietly paid for work they did not do, and someone else quietly is not. How long has that been true?

The company's rules, its tiers, its own logic, and a clawback that can never take back more than was earned. Ezra keeps the company's word to its people, every month, without a conversation.

Two paths. One summit.
03

Ten staff. Or none.

A one-person company carries the same clients, the same follow-ups, the same month-end as a floor of thirty, with nobody to hand the binder to. Ezra is the back office not yet hired. It tracks what ten people would, and it never calls in sick.

Pegasus

The real number, finally still.

A business earns what it earns and spends what it spends. The hard part has always been finding out. Receipts arrive through one door, invoices through another, somebody needs money on a Tuesday, and months later a number has to be right and the paper is somewhere else. What did the last guess cost? Pegasus was built inside operating companies so an owner never answers that question with a shrug.

01

The CPA you can afford.

A company does not have to be big to have its numbers straight. It has to stop guessing.

Nothing lost. Nothing hidden.
