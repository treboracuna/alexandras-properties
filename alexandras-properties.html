The Commercial Wing

Named for a daughter.
Built for the Valley.

Alexandra's Properties is the commercial side of the Acuña family — plazas, venues, and gathering places in Edinburg and across the Rio Grande Valley. The split is simple: Exclusiva builds where people live. Alexandra's builds where the Valley works, gathers, and celebrates.

The name is Jennifer Alexandra's — a promise made a generation ago, kept in brick. Every property that carries it is held and operated by the family, not sold and left behind. We only build what we would own — and what carries her name, we keep.

Alexandra's Plaza — Edinburg

Halfway up and nearly spoken for. Alexandra's Plaza is six commercial suites at 1112 W Montecristo in Edinburg, Texas — stucco going up now, and five of the six suites already leased before completion. One suite remains, approximately 550 square feet.

W Montecristo is one of Edinburg's busiest corridors — if you've driven it, you know. Suites lease directly from the owner. No management company in another state, no layers — the people who decide are the people you can drive to.
