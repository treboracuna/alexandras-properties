## 8. BRAND VOICE CONTRACTS — VIOLATIONS ARE REJECTIONS

**Exclusiva / Alexandra's Properties**
- Legacy over lifestyle. Raw construction plus vision. Community pride.
- **Banned phrases:** nestled, luxury living redefined, unparalleled, boasts, stunning, dream home, state-of-the-art, elevate your lifestyle. No square-footage-and-amenities recitation. Nothing that reads like a listing.
- Black-gold branding already exists. Evolution, not rebrand.

**Texaswide Insurance**
- Trust over transactions. Education over pressure. Community over commission.
- **Banned phrases:** don't get caught, act now, limited time, before it's too late, running out, you could lose, protect yourself before. No manufactured urgency, including during AEP.
- Bilingual is native. EN/ES parity.

**Acuna Financial / electronic tax filing**
- Precision and discretion. Quiet competence — the people who handle it correctly, every year, without drama.
- **Banned:** refund-size hype, "maximize your refund," IRS fear framing, any guarantee of outcome.

**ACUÑA umbrella**
- The family name is the through-line. The Rio Grande Valley is the ground, not a demographic target.
