Ezra

What would it feel like to know?

Not hope. Not probably fine. Know. Every agency principal, and every agent working alone, knows the quiet weight of a statement that is technically complete and possibly wrong. How much has that weight cost you over the years, and who would ever tell you? Ezra was built inside an agency to set it down for good.

01

Statements in. Answers out.

Every carrier you write, every statement, every export lands in one place and gets held against the policies you actually wrote. Three answers come back: what matched, what needs a look, and what got taken back. The fog over the month lifts, and what is left is a number you can stand on.

Nothing is trusted until it matches a policy you wrote. Not the carrier's total. Not the spreadsheet. Not the feeling that it is probably fine.

The fog lifts
02

Who earned it. Who got paid for it.

Carriers know who a policy was written under. They do not know who sat at the kitchen table and earned it. If those two names are ever different in your agency, someone is quietly getting paid for work they did not do, and someone else is quietly not. How long has that been true?

Your rules, your tiers, your household logic, chargebacks that can never take back more than an agent earned. Ezra keeps your word to your people, every month, without a conversation.

Two paths. One summit.
03

Ten staff. Or none.

A one-person agency carries the same carriers, the same statements, the same chargebacks as a floor of thirty, with nobody to hand the binder to. Ezra is the back office you have not hired yet. It tracks what ten people would, and it never calls in sick.

Built for the agent who is the whole agency, and for the principal who runs one and wants the number before the carrier calls.

Pegasus

The real number, finally still.

A building costs what it costs. The hard part has always been finding out. Pegasus was built on real job sites so an owner never answers that question with a shrug.

01

The CPA you can afford.

You do not have to be big to have your numbers straight. You just have to stop guessing.

02

Nothing lost. Nothing hidden.

Every receipt, invoice, and advance is kept the moment it is handed over, and a wrong entry is never erased, only set aside where you can still see it. Snap the slip, keep the slip, move on. Years from now, the answer is still there.

Transparency is not a report you run. It is how the ledger is built. Every layer stays.
