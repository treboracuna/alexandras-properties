The Build

Built to hold.
Not to flip.

Alexandra's Plaza is the second property to carry her name. Six commercial suites at 1112 W Montecristo, on one of the busiest corridors in Edinburg.

The frame is up. The stucco is going on. Five of the six suites were leased before the walls were finished. That is not a sales line. That is what happens when a building goes up on the right road with a landlord you can look in the eye.

Here is what it means when the name is on it. The owner is the family. No management company in another state. No layers between a tenant and a decision. The person who signs the lease is the person who answers the phone. We only build what we would own, and what carries her name, we keep.
